Understanding Fraud

Fraud generally involves deliberate deception or misrepresentation intended to obtain an unlawful or unfair advantage.

Overview

Fraud can occur in personal, commercial and online transactions. It may involve false statements, concealment of important information, forged documents or other deceptive conduct.

The specific legal consequences depend on the facts of the matter and the applicable law.

Common Forms of Fraud

Fraudulent conduct can take different forms depending on the circumstances.

  • Online and investment scams
  • Fraudulent business transactions
  • False representations
  • Forgery and use of false documents
  • Fraud involving financial transactions

Evidence

Evidence can be important when investigating or responding to an allegation of fraud.

Depending on the circumstances, relevant material may include contracts, receipts, messages, emails, transaction records, photographs and other documents.

Reporting Suspected Fraud

A person who believes they have been defrauded should consider preserving relevant records and obtaining appropriate professional advice.

The appropriate reporting or legal route will depend on the nature of the suspected conduct.

Legal Proceedings

Fraud-related matters may involve civil proceedings, criminal investigations, criminal proceedings, or a combination of legal processes depending on the facts.

The available remedies and procedures depend on the circumstances and applicable Nigerian law.

Seeking Legal Assistance

Anyone involved in a fraud-related dispute should consider obtaining advice from a qualified legal practitioner who can assess the particular facts of the matter.
